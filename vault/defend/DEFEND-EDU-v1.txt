- Tak percaya: Evidence first — biar bukti yang bercakap
  Evidence first — let proof speak
- Ada tempat lain: Differentiation — tunjukkan beza yang meaningful
  Show meaningful difference, never badmouth competitor
- Tak urgent: Consequence framing — tunjukkan kos tunggu (fakta sahaja)
  Show cost of waiting (facts only, no fake urgency
- Kena tanya keluarga: Include decision maker — jadikan mereka sekutu
  Include decision maker — make them an ally
